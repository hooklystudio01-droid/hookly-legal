# Hookly Publication - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

## 1. Who we are
Hookly Publication is a personal tool operated by Hookly Studio (contact: hooklystudio01@gmail.com). It is used only by its owner to publish the owner's own short videos to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

## 2. What data we access
When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and the basic profile information needed to post videos (the account's display name and the posting options available to it). We do not access, collect or store any information about other TikTok users, their followers, their messages or their videos.

## 3. How data is used
The access token is used only to upload and publish videos that the owner has created, with the title and hashtags the owner has approved. It is stored locally on the owner's own computer and is never shared, sold or transferred to third parties.

## 4. Data retention and deletion
Tokens remain on the owner's computer until they are deleted. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites) and delete the stored token, which immediately ends all access.

## 5. Children
The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from them.

## 6. Contact
For any question about this policy, write to hooklystudio01@gmail.com.
